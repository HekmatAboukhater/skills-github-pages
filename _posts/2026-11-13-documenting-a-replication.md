---
title: "Documenting a Replication"
date: 2026-11-13
---

A replication README should tell a reader what question the analysis asks, where its data come from, and how to regenerate its figures or tables.

For an R project, I would start with a short run order, list package versions, and explain any judgment calls about sample restrictions or variable coding. For example, the [AQRD quantitative portfolio](https://github.com/HekmatAboukhater/aqrd-quantitative-portfolio) pairs code with notes on the research design.
